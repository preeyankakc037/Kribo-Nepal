# 🚨 Problem Statement

Nepal's agricultural supply chain is heavily dependent on local middlemen and broker monopolies. Farmers often have limited access to buyers, little bargaining power, and minimal transparency regarding market prices. They are forced to accept whatever price is offered because reaching multiple brokers is difficult and time-consuming.

Some of the major challenges include:

- Farmers have very limited choices when selling their produce.
- Broker monopolies reduce competition and suppress fair pricing.
- There is no centralized marketplace connecting farmers and brokers.
- Price negotiations lack transparency.
- Farmers have little visibility into nearby brokers who may offer better deals.
- Brokers also struggle to discover farmers with available produce in different regions.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

**Kribo Nepal** is a digital agricultural marketplace that removes information barriers between farmers and brokers.

Farmers can list their available produce, while verified brokers can post their purchasing requirements. Instead of relying on personal networks or a single middleman, farmers gain access to multiple potential buyers in one place.

The platform enables:

- Farmers to reach multiple brokers.
- Brokers to discover farmers across different locations.
- Location-based discovery for both farmers and brokers.
- Transparent communication and negotiations.
- Better market accessibility throughout Nepal.

As the platform grows, Kribo Nepal aims to transform agricultural trading into a transparent, competitive, and technology-driven ecosystem.

# 🤖 AI Vision

Kribo Nepal is designed with AI-powered features that will continuously improve the marketplace.

Planned AI capabilities include:

- 📈 Future crop price prediction using historical market trends.
- 💰 Market price forecasting to help farmers decide the best time to sell.
- 📍 Smart broker recommendations based on location and trading history.
- 🌾 Personalized crop recommendations based on regional demand.
- 📊 Agricultural market insights and analytics.

Our long-term vision is to provide farmers with data-driven insights instead of relying solely on traditional market practices.
